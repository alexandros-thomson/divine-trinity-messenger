Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Kypria LLC ("Kypria Studios," "we," "our," or "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you interact with the Divine Trinity Messenger chatbot ("the Service") through Facebook Messenger.

Information We Collect

When you interact with the Divine Trinity Messenger via Facebook Messenger, we collect:

  • Facebook Page-Scoped User ID (PSID) — a unique identifier assigned by Facebook for your interaction with our Page. This is not your personal Facebook profile ID and cannot be used to identify you on Facebook.
  • Message Content — the text messages you send to the Divine Trinity Messenger.
  • Conversation History — recent messages (up to 6) are temporarily stored to provide contextual AI responses within a single conversation thread.
  • Usage Data — timestamps and frequency of interactions for managing the free-tier message limit (3 messages per day).

How We Use Your Information

We use collected information solely to:

  • Respond to your messages with AI-generated content from the Divine Trinity personas (Zeus, Aphrodite, Lifesphere).
  • Maintain conversation context for coherent multi-turn interactions.
  • Enforce the freemium usage limit (3 free messages per day).
  • Process Premium subscription payments through Stripe if you choose to upgrade.

Third-Party Services

The Service uses the following third-party providers:

  • Meta (Facebook) — to receive and send messages via the Messenger Platform.
  • OpenAI — to generate AI responses. Your messages are sent to OpenAI's API for processing. OpenAI's data usage policy applies to these interactions.
  • Stripe — to process Premium subscription payments. Stripe's privacy policy governs payment data.
  • Supabase — to securely store user and conversation data with row-level security enabled.

Data Retention

  • Conversation history is retained for contextual response quality.
  • Usage logs are retained for freemium enforcement.
  • You may request deletion of all your data at any time by contacting us.

Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including:

  • Row-Level Security (RLS) on all database tables.
  • Encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) for all data in transit.
  • Environment variable isolation for all API credentials.
  • Stripe webhook signature verification for payment events.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to your stored data.
  • Request deletion of your data.
  • Stop interacting with the Service at any time by simply not messaging the Page.
  • Cancel your Premium subscription at any time through Stripe.

Children's Privacy

The Service is not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
